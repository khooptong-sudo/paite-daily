# Paite Daily — Starter Video

## Style Prompt

Monochrome, editorial, quiet. White paper, black ink, one glass card. The only
colour in the frame is the puandum band, because the band is the cultural identity.
Motion is calm and confident: slow reveals, no bounce, no spin. The site's real
interface is the hero image, not a drawing of it.

## Colors

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Paper | `#ffffff` |
| Ink | `#000000` |
| Muted body | `rgba(0,0,0,.65)` |
| Glass surface | `rgba(0,0,0,.035)` |
| Hairline | `rgba(0,0,0,.12)` |
| Device frame | `#0b0b0b` |
| Band (identity only) | `#00a651` green, `#f6ff00` yellow, `#b0102e` red, `#1725d4` blue, `#ec008c` pink |

## Typography

- Display: `Young Serif` — titles and the sentence in the card.
- Body: `Atkinson Hyperlegible` — labels, steps, captions.
- Labels are uppercase, 0.12em tracked, 700 weight.

## Motion Rules

1. Entrances only, except the transition crossfade and the final fade-out.
2. One idea per scene. Never two things move at once at full strength.
3. The phone is one object for the whole middle of the film. It enters once and leaves once.
4. Eases vary per element: `power3.out`, `power2.out`, `expo.out`, `power2.inOut`.
5. Nothing loops forever. The caret blinks a finite number of times.

## What NOT to Do

- No gradients across the whole frame, no neon, no drop-shadow glow.
- No stock-photo people, no hands, no stock music.
- No invented Paite text. Only real strings from the repository.
- No camera shake, no zoom punch, no kinetic type stacking.
