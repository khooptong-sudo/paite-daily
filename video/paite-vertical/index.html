Paite Daily

The people writing Paite into the future.

One sentence a day. No account. No cost.

What it is

One English sentence a day.

The site shows a simple English sentence. You write the same sentence in Paite.

It takes about a minute. Your answer helps keep the language alive.

How to use it

1Read the English sentence.
2Type it in Paite.A note helps the reviewer. The note is optional.
3Tap Send.

That is one contribution. Come back tomorrow for a new sentence.

What happens next

Reviewers check every answer.

The checked sentences join an open dataset. Everyone can use it, free, under CC0.
