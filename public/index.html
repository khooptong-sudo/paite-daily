    Nampuan Liangbat Gat Your woven shawl

    • What it is. A shawl woven from your Paite. Each sentence or word you give, once a reviewer verifies it, adds one thread.
    • How it is woven. Your name picks one of three nampuan: Puandum, BA / Siamsin Puan or Ngoutekhelh. The colours are that shawl's own. The order of the bands is yours alone.
    • What it does. It grows as you give. Tap Share to post it as your WhatsApp status.
