    Speed and behaviour

    No cookies, and no IP address is stored. Each person is counted once a day with a code that changes every day, so nobody can be followed from one day to the next. A city, phone model, network or time zone is named once two or more visitors share it; otherwise it counts under Other. Visits are kept 30 days, daily totals for good. Browsers that ask not to be tracked are not counted.

    Shortcuts: J and K (or the arrow keys, or 1 to 9) move between answers. A or Enter accepts the highlighted answer, X rejects it only, E edits it before accepting (Esc to leave the box), V ticks it as a variant. S saves every answer as a variant, N skips this sentence, R rejects all, U undoes your last decision, ? shows or hides this help. On a phone, swipe an answer right to accept it, left to reject it. Each answer also has its own Reject button, which leaves the other answers open. Skip leaves the sentence in the queue for another reviewer and does not show it to you again for two hours.
